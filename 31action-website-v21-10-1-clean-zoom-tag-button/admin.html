Workflow

What this version does

1. Creates the gallery in D1 as a draft.   2. Uploads each full-resolution original privately to R2.   3. Creates a smaller watermarked preview in your browser and uploads it to the preview bucket.   4. Saves each photo record in D1.   5. Publishes the gallery when all selected photographs finish successfully.
