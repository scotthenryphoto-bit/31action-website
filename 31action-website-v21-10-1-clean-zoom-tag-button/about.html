Behind the Camera

Photography that feels like you were there.

I’m Scott Henry, a Maryland-based photographer focused on sports, portraits, events and visual storytelling. I look for the split-second expression, the decisive play, the unexpected light and the small moments happening around the main event.

31 ACTION comes from the idea that life happens 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. 24 + 7 = 31. The camera should be ready when the moment happens.

From high-school athletics and live events to portraits, aviation and cityscapes, the goal is the same: make strong images that hold onto the feeling of the moment.
